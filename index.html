Two arms, a mobile base, and the computer on board.

An open-source, two-arm robot you can build for $1,247 in parts. Its Jetson computer rides on board, so it learns tasks and runs them on battery, with no laptop attached.

What it can do

Two arms, driven by you or by a learned policy

Drive both arms from a VR headset, record what you do, and train the robot to repeat it. Recording, training, and running all happen on the robot.

What's new

What changed from XLeRobot

XLeRobot runs its learned policies on a separate laptop. XLeRobot-Pro puts the computer on board, and the full robot runs on its own, without brownouts.

  • Computer on boardA Jetson Orin Nano Super records, trains, and runs policies on the robot.
  • Runs on its own, without brownoutsThe arms have their own power outlet and no longer share servo buses with the head and wheels.
  • Thicker printed linksLoad-bearing arm links have thicker walls, so they carry the added load.
